2. Description of Service#

Invisible String is a mobile application that helps couples discover moments when they may have been in the same place before they met. The App:

  • Scans photo metadata (location and timestamp) from your device's photo library (with your permission)
  • Allows you to securely share location data with a connected partner via nearby connection (Bluetooth/WiFi) or invite code
  • Identifies potential "near-miss" encounters in three categories:
    • Chance Encounters: Times you were in the same place on the same day
    • Closest Distance: Your nearest moments ever, regardless of time
    • Closest in Time: Synchronized moments, regardless of distance
  • Presents results in an interactive story format with shareable images
  • The App is intended for couples who mutually consent to share location history with each other for entertainment purposes. It is not designed for, and must not be used for, surveillance, stalking, or sharing another person's data without their full knowledge and consent.

5. Privacy and Data#

Your privacy is important to us. Please review our Privacy Policy, which explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Key points:

  • Photo scanning and matching occurs entirely on your device
  • Matching uses only extracted location metadata. Photos you choose to share with your partner are sent directly between your devices and never pass through our servers
  • Location data shared between partners is end-to-end encrypted using ChaChaPoly encryption
